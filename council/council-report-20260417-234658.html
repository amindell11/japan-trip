Where the council agrees High confidence

  1. Sakura Cross's "1 room, 2 double beds for 4" is a bed-config problem. Four advisors flagged it independently — two straight male friends sharing a Japanese 140cm double is an unresolved assumption. Only the Expansionist glossed over it.
  2. Akihabara is the wrong base for the Tokyo arrival leg. Day 3 (Takao via Keio Line) and Day 4 (Kawaguchi bus) both depart Shinjuku. All five advisors agreed, by different routes.
  3. Eslead Namba South III wins Osaka. Namba is the correct transit node for Dotonbori, Osaka Castle, and USJ.

Where the council clashes Real tradeoff

Tokyo arrival — ryokan vs business hotel

The Expansionist pushed Onyado Nono Asakusa Villa ($1,280 trip) — a ryokan 150m from Senso-ji with onsen, for "a core memory." Every other advisor went Sakura Cross Shinjuku ($440). The Expansionist's take is defensible if you're willing to violate the user's stated "price and closeness" priority for a once-in-a-lifetime experience. But it also loads two painful morning commutes from Asakusa to Shinjuku on Days 3–4 in rainy season with gear. Verdict: budget wins; if splurging, splurge in Kyoto not Tokyo.

Kyoto — Aoi Nakamoto guesthouse vs APA Gion hotel

Only the Expansionist wanted Aoi. The others pointed out: shared bath + futons-on-floor after Takao hike + camping + Fushimi climbing is real physical friction in rainy season, plus a couple sharing a communal bathhouse with strangers is unflagged discomfort. Verdict: APA Gion, connecting rooms gives the couple privacy and saves the group's legs.

Blind spots the council caught on peer review Missed initially

  1. The June 10–12 calendar gap. Kyoto ends Jun 10, Osaka starts Jun 12 — that's 2 unaccounted nights (presumably Kasagi + Hiyoshi/GRAX camping). No one priced a rain-out fallback during tsuyu season.
  2. Luggage logistics across camping nights. 4 people × 2 camping nights = where do suitcases live? Takkyubin / takuhaibin (luggage forwarding, ~¥2,000/bag) from Tokyo → Kyoto and Kyoto → Osaka sidesteps this entirely and makes the neighborhood question matter less.
  3. Japanese hotel 4-per-room legality. Many Japanese hotels legally cap rooms at 2–3 guests. Confirm the specific room config — some hotels add surcharges or require a second room for the 4th person.
  4. Day 15 departure airport — Narita or Haneda? Determines whether Super Hotel Akihabara is actually convenient for the return. If Haneda, pick something on the Yamanote south side near Shinagawa instead.
  5. Rakuten availability expires. The returned prices are live quotes. Lock in the bookings soon — especially APA Gion, reopened March 2026, and likely to fill for summer.

The full recommendation

Take the Executor's plan (Response B). It's the only response that actually commits, prices, and sequences the bookings. The Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, and Outsider correctly diagnose the bed-config + Akihabara-neighborhood problems; the Executor acts on those diagnoses.

One deviation from B: confirm Day 15 departure airport before locking Super Hotel Akihabara. If Haneda, switch to a Shinagawa-area hotel (not in the current shortlist — rerun the fetch script with Shinagawa center).

Add one line item the council all missed: book takkyubin luggage forwarding from Tokyo → Kyoto (before Day 4 camp) and Kyoto → Osaka (before Day 7 camp). ~¥8,000 total for 4 bags. This decouples neighborhood choice from luggage logistics entirely.
